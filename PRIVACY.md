# Kindler Dictionary privacy policy

Kindler Dictionary sends the text you select to https://kindler.ink only when you choose **Translate "…" in Kindler** from the right-click menu. kindler.ink uses it to look up the word and send back its dictionary entry.

- Nothing is sent without that click.
- Only the selected text is sent: no browsing history, no other page content, no identifiers.
- The add-on stores nothing and has no analytics.
- The add-on cannot read pages on its own; it runs on a page only after you use its menu item there.

Questions: https://github.com/kindlerz/KindlerDictionary/issues
